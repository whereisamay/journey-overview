Commission Analytics Agent

Partners had a number of queries about their commission calculations.

40–45weekly support queries removed
15–20active partners
Ledgerthe only source of every number shown

The problem

Finmo's partners earn a share of the fees on every merchant they bring in. With many merchants, different pricing plans and several currencies, partners got a payout figure but couldn't see how it was built. So they asked support, 40 to 45 times a week.

What we built

First, a per-merchant breakdown: the pricing plan applied, Finmo's margin, the partner's share and the USD payout after FX. It came with an Excel export, because partners reconcile in their own spreadsheets.

Then an AI layer on top. Partners could ask questions about their commissions in plain language, question a trend, and find the insight that mattered to their business.

What a partner sees

Partner workspace · Ask your dataDummy data
Why is my commission lower this cycle? My volume was about the same.
Your total volume was the same, $2.9M. What changed is the mix: far less of it went through FX conversions, and FX earns you the most commission per dollar.

Source: commission ledger, payout cycles 21 and 22
So the drop is all FX?
Yes. Last cycle, FX conversions were 31% of your volume. This cycle they were 14%. Pays and payouts grew, but they earn far less per dollar, so they couldn't make up the gap.
